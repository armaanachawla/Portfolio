# MAYA — Wellness for your next chapter

**A nutritionist-led everyday wellness concept for women in perimenopause and menopause.**

MAYA was developed as an early-stage venture concept focused on making midlife women's wellness more approachable through everyday routines and thoughtfully designed nutrition products.

## My role: Commercial Strategy & Business Development
- Customer research and synthesis of women's wellness needs.
- Competitor research and positioning.
- Market sizing and business-case development.
- Product range and proposition development.
- Pricing, unit economics and go-to-market planning.
- Pitch development.

The venture was developed and pitched at the Masters’ Union MBA Summer School National Startup Competition, where it was selected as the winner among 50 competing teams from across India.
