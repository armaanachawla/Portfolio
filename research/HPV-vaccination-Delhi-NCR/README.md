# HPV vaccination attitudes and cultural barriers in Delhi-NCR

**Research paper · November 2019**

**Title:** *Surveying Clinical Attitudes, Perceptions, and the Effect of Cultural Barriers Towards the Usage of Human Papillomavirus Vaccines in Delhi-NCR*

**Author:** Armaana Chawla  
**Publication:** International Journal of Social Science and Economic Research, Volume 04, Issue 11 (November 2019).

## Overview
This paper reports a survey of 33 healthcare providers in Delhi-NCR on attitudes toward HPV vaccination and the role of cultural beliefs and taboos.
